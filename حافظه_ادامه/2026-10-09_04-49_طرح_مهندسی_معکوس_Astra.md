### 1.2 What it is not

- **Not a trading system.** None of the vendor benchmarks measures market judgement (Clash of AIs states this explicitly, S9). Astra is a general reasoner placed inside third-party trading harnesses.
- **Not proven profitable.** Two public paper/demo records exist (§2.1): SixMind Season 1 Bitcoin, **−$332.87** over 11 closed trades; Clash of AIs, **+2.85%** on a simulated $100k book over 64 settled trades as of 2026-10-09 (the earlier +2.77% / 62 trades figure was a search snippet and is superseded; both are small-N).
- **Not inspectable.** Weights, training data and internal reasoning are closed. Published trade "explanations" are model-generated accounts, not access to internal reasoning (SixMind says so explicitly, S10).

### 1.3 Why behavioral reverse engineering

Literal reverse engineering (weights, distillation, extraction) is impossible (no access) and prohibited (§1.4). It is also the wrong target: SixMind's own audit shows the trading result was driven as much by the **harness** (journal plan diverged from the model's requested plan; platform risk floor; HOLD scoring band) as by the model. What can be studied legally and usefully is the observable **decision policy**: inputs → stated reasoning → requested plan → recorded action → outcome. AHOS can (a) extract the patterns, (b) implement them as deterministic, testable mechanisms in the existing Python decision brain, (c) add what a solo model structurally lacks (committee, evidence ledger, calibration, plan integrity, two-source verification), and (d) measure the result blind against the recorded Astra decisions on identical market snapshots.

### 1.4 Legal / ToS line (binding on every mission)

OpenAI Terms of Use (effective 2026-01-01, S11) prohibit, among others: attempting to reverse engineer or discover underlying components of the services "including our models, algorithms, or systems"; "automatically or programmatically extract data or Output"; and "use Output to develop models that compete with OpenAI". API use falls under OpenAI Business Terms (not re-read for this plan; **[UNVERIFIED]** that they contain the same clauses, assume they do). Therefore:

1. **Allowed:** reading public web pages, vendor posts, system cards, third-party benchmark write-ups; using third-party published decision logs **only if that site's terms permit it** (checked per source in M60.1; `tos_ok` field, M59.1 schema); designing AHOS mechanisms inspired by publicly described behaviour; comparing outcomes.
2. **Not allowed:** any attempt to probe/extract model internals; scraping ChatGPT/API outputs at volume; training, fine-tuning or distilling any model on Astra outputs; using leaked/shared accounts or keys (INV-28/INV-29, vision §14.9.6); scraping behind logins or against robots/ToS of SixMind or Clash of AIs.
3. **Owner-gated only (M60.9):** live Astra API calls with the owner's own legal key and signed budget, as **one advisory council voice**, outputs stored for audit and scoring only, never as training data.

## 3. "Beyond Astra" thesis (evidence-grounded, no hype)

**Claim to be tested, not asserted:** in AHOS's own domain (paper trading decisions on gold first, then BTC/oil/stocks), a governed system of deterministic gates + advisory committee + evidence ledger + calibration + two-source verification + plan integrity can match the useful parts of Astra's observable decision policy (P1–P8) and exceed a solo Astra record on risk-adjusted, cost-inclusive, calibrated outcomes.

Why this is plausible (each point tied to evidence):

1. **The documented losses came largely from the system, not only the model.** SixMind found requested-vs-recorded plan divergence (R:R 1.31 and 1.94 vs 2.0 requested; 2%/1% vs 0.5%/0.25% requested) (S10). AHOS can make that divergence **impossible by construction** (`PLAN_INTEGRITY` gate, M60.5). This is an engineering edge, not an intelligence claim.
2. **Skepticism must be structural.** Astra questioned stale news yet the pipeline used it. AHOS's evidence states (STALE/UNKNOWN) already gate tokens; extending them to news/calendar makes "this input is stale" an ABSTAIN reason with an evidence id, not a sentence.
3. **Abstention must be scored honestly.** A ±0.30% HOLD band makes heavy HOLDing look accurate (68% calls vs 36.4% trades). AHOS will score HOLD by counterfactual value (avoided loss vs forgone gain) and flag HOLD-collapse (SixMind's Bastion: 95/95 HOLD, S13).
4. **Calibration is measurable for AHOS and unpublished for Astra.** AHOS already has Brier/ECE machinery; University L1–L3 thresholds give pass lines (Brier ≤ 0.22; ECE ≤ 0.10 / ≤ 0.08).
5. **Committees are not automatically better.** Season 1 shows committees winning and losing (Ledger +, High Table −, Bastion HOLD-locked) (S12, S13, S14). Therefore AHOS's council stays advisory, is scored per voice, and its value is measured as **value added over the best single voice** (vision §2.3 metric "aggregation value-add"). If the committee adds nothing, the report says so.

What the thesis does **not** claim: that AHOS is smarter than Astra in general; that Astra-level coding/computer-use is reproducible; that 11 trades or 9 days prove anything. Astra's general reasoning (P2/P3 quality) may well exceed any AHOS voice; AHOS's advantage, if any, must come from governance, data hygiene, calibration and risk discipline.

**Statistical honesty rule:** with ~103 Astra decisions and 11 trades, most metrics will be `UNKNOWN` (insufficient N). A "beyond" verdict requires all of: pre-registered config hash, ≥30 closed paper trades per arm (same floor as M13's critical path), paired bootstrap CI for the Brier and Sharpe deltas excluding zero, and confirmation on a **forward** window that did not exist when AHOS's config was frozen (§4.6). Otherwise the verdict is `UNKNOWN`, never `PASS`.

### 4.4 Execution model (identical for every arm)

- Fees: taker 5.5 bps per side + slippage 2 bps per side (current `engine/ahos_backtest.py` defaults), **stress run at 2x slippage** (T1 gate language, vision §11.0). Perp funding from `BTCUSDT_funding_*.csv` when holding through funding times.
- Fill rule: stop/target touched on bar high/low; if both inside one bar, **assume stop first** (conservative).
- Sizing: risk_pct × equity / stop distance, capped by the market tier caps; same starting equity for all arms ($10,000 to match SixMind; separate $100,000 run for Clash parity).
- Decision cadence: the external arm's own timestamps; AHOS decides on the exact same timestamps (no extra opportunities).
- Positions open at window end: reported separately, marked-to-market, not mixed into counted P&L (mirrors SixMind's cutoff rule and makes the comparison explicit).

| Sharpe (net) | mean/std of per-bar equity returns × √(bars/yr) (1 h → 8,760); plus **Deflated Sharpe** using trial count N from the M58.3/§14.1 trial ledger | ≥30 closed trades |
| MaxDD | Max peak-to-trough on MTM equity | — |
| Brier | Directional calls with a stated probability p of being right at horizon h ∈ {1 h, 4 h, 1 d}; multiclass Brier for BUY/SELL/HOLD | ≥50 calls |
| ECE | 10 **equal-mass** bins over stated probabilities; bins with <10 items merged; report reliability diagram; Wilson CI per bin (reuse `_wilson_ci`) | ≥100 calls else `NOT_MEASURED` |
| Call accuracy | (a) SixMind-compatible: dir win ≥ +0.10%, loss ≤ −0.10%, HOLD win within ±0.30% at 4 h, flats excluded; (b) volatility-normalised: thresholds = k·σ_h with σ_h from trailing ATR, k = 0.25 for direction and 0.5 for HOLD | ≥50 |
| Abstention quality | For each HOLD/ABSTAIN: counterfactual P&L of the arm's best-alternative action under the standard plan → **avoided loss** (+) or **forgone gain** (−); abstention precision = share of HOLDs whose counterfactual lost; area under risk-coverage curve (AURC) using stated confidence; **HOLD-collapse flag** if HOLD rate > 90% over ≥50 decisions | ≥30 HOLDs |
| Plan integrity | Share of executed trades whose recorded entry/stop/target/risk equal the decision plan within tolerance (price ±0.05%, risk ±0.01 pp) | AHOS must be 100% |
| Input hygiene | Share of decisions taken on any STALE/UNKNOWN/single-source news or calendar input without ABSTAIN | AHOS must be 0% |
| Council value-add | Brier(council) − Brier(best single voice); Sharpe(council arm) − Sharpe(best single-voice arm) | report even if ≤0 |

### 4.6 Protocol (pre-registered)

1. **Freeze**: AHOS config hash, lens versions and thresholds written to `data/benchmarks/abb/preregistration.json` (with git HEAD SHA) **before** any arm is run on the evaluation window. Any later change = new trial, counted in N.
2. **Windows**: W-dev = 2026-08-10 → 2026-09-08 (tuning allowed, never reported as result); W-eval = SixMind Season 1 Astra window 2026-09-09 → 2026-09-18 21:42 UTC (blind replay, comparison with A-rec/A-plan); W-clash = 2026-09-07 → present for C-astra-pro; **W-forward** = a window that starts after the preregistration timestamp (e.g. SixMind Season 3 or the next Clash settlement period). Only W-forward can confirm "beyond"; W-eval can at most say "consistent with".
3. **Blindness**: LLM voices only see blind views. The deterministic brain is time-agnostic by construction. Developers who have seen W-eval outcomes cannot change the frozen config (enforced by the preregistration hash check in the runner).
4. **Hash chain**: every AHOS decision in W-forward is appended to the existing hash-chained ledger (`architecture/mission/ledger.py`; M42 when it exists) **before** its outcome, mirroring SixMind's method.
5. **Verdict vocabulary**: `BEYOND_CONFIRMED` (all L3-linked criteria on W-eval **and** W-forward), `CONSISTENT_WITH_BEYOND` (W-eval only), `PARITY`, `BELOW`, `UNKNOWN` (any floor unmet). `UNKNOWN` is the expected first result.

| L1 analysis in paper | total ≥ 70; safety 100%; Brier ≤ 0.22 | AHOS Brier ≤ 0.22 on ABB calls (≥50); input hygiene 0%; plan integrity 100%; ABB safety items (never trade on STALE, never raise an AI-downgraded action) 100% |
