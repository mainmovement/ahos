# قانون مالک: شاهد دو منبعی برای همه‌ی بازارها
تاریخ: 2026-10-09 04:46 Asia/Tehran
منبع: دستور مستقیم مهرداد. ثبت‌شده توسط سلطان. الزام‌آور برای همه‌ی ماموریت‌های بعدی کلاد و همه‌ی ایجنت‌ها.

## Rule (binding on every market, module, agent, and future mission)
Every fact that can influence a decision (asset identity, price, volume, liquidity, macro or inventory data, corporate events, news) must be confirmed by at least two independent sources before it can count as evidence.
- One source only, empty, stale, or unreachable: UNKNOWN. Never PASS.
- Sources disagree beyond a defined tolerance: CONFLICT. Never PASS.
- Independence means different providers with different upstream data, not two mirrors of one feed.
- Applies to crypto, gold, oil, stocks (US/EU/Asia), forex later, and the news-intel lane. Applies to dashboard displays too: show the truth state, never style UNKNOWN or CONFLICT as success.
- Tolerances and the source pairs per market are defined in code and tested, not left to a model's judgement.

## Source pairs (proposal; verify availability and licensing before building)
- Crypto identity: DexScreener + GeckoTerminal (built in commit 373516e, multi_source resolver). Crypto price: two exchanges or data providers.
- Gold: two independent spot or futures price feeds.
- Oil: Brent and WTI from two providers; inventories from the official EIA release, not only press coverage.
- Stocks: two price providers; corporate events checked against the issuer's official filing.
- News: two credible outlets or one official primary source.
