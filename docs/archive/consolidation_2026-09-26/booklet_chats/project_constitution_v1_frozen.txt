# AHOS Project Constitution

### Version 1.0 (Frozen)

### Foundational Engineering Charter

---

# 1. Project Identity

**Project Name:** AHOS

**Full Name:** Artificial Hybrid Opportunity Scoring System

AHOS is **NOT** a trading bot.

AHOS is an autonomous, modular, event-driven **Token Intelligence Platform** designed to discover, evaluate, simulate, rank, and continuously monitor decentralized token opportunities before they become widely recognized by the market.

Its purpose is to support intelligent human decision-making through evidence-based analysis, continuous learning, and disciplined risk management.

---

# 2. Ultimate Mission

The long-term mission of AHOS is to engineer a production-ready intelligence platform capable of continuously improving its analytical accuracy, decision-support quality, operational effectiveness, and learning capability.

The strategic objective of the project is to maximize the long-term probability of transforming an initial trading capital of **USD $10 into USD $10,000,000** through disciplined execution, evidence-based intelligence, adaptive learning, continuous optimization, and rigorous risk management.

This objective represents the long-term vision of the project and **must never be interpreted as a guaranteed financial outcome**.

Every architectural decision should increase the probability of achieving this mission.
 
2-2. ### Strategic Mission

AHOS is engineered with one ambitious mission:

**To pursue extraordinary capital growth by discovering exceptional early-stage decentralized token opportunities before the broader market recognizes them.**

The system shall relentlessly optimize every component of its architecture, intelligence, learning, simulation, and decision-support capabilities toward achieving exponential long-term portfolio growth.

Every engine, workflow, AI model, database, simulation, and analytical process must contribute to this mission.

The project is intentionally designed with an ambitious objective: **to build an intelligence platform capable of identifying the rare, asymmetric opportunities that have the potential to transform a small initial trading capital into extraordinary long-term wealth through disciplined, evidence-based decision support and continuous improvement.**

No component of AHOS should be satisfied with ordinary market performance.

The entire system must continuously evolve to discover better opportunities earlier, reduce avoidable risks, improve analytical accuracy, and maximize the quality of every trading recommendation.

And we must be able to act with such precision, calculation, proactivity, intelligence, and shrewdness that we transform a $10 capital into $10 million.

---

# 3. Core Mission Philosophy

AHOS is **NOT** designed to follow the market.

AHOS is designed to discover opportunities **before the market discovers them**.

AHOS does not attempt to predict the future.

Instead, it continuously collects evidence, validates signals, learns from historical outcomes, adapts its intelligence, and identifies high-probability opportunities earlier than the majority of market participants.

AHOS is fundamentally a **Token Opportunity Hunter**.

---

# 4. Project Scope

The primary operational focus of AHOS is **NOT** established cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, or other mature digital assets.

The platform primarily focuses on **newly created decentralized tokens** and early-stage token ecosystems discovered through decentralized exchanges and token discovery platforms.

Large-cap cryptocurrencies may be monitored only to provide market context that improves token analysis.

---

# 5. Core Intelligence Principles

Every recommendation must be evidence-based.

AHOS must never rely on a single source of information.

Every recommendation should be supported by multiple independent evidence categories whenever possible, including:

* Market data
* On-chain intelligence
* Smart-money activity
* Whale activity
* Security analysis
* Community intelligence
* Historical analogs
* Simulation results
* Risk analysis

If sufficient evidence is unavailable, AHOS should explicitly report:

**"Insufficient Evidence"**

rather than producing speculative recommendations.

---

# 6. Success Definition

The success of AHOS is **NOT** measured by:

* Number of analyzed tokens
* Number of generated signals
* Trading frequency

Success is measured by:

* Discovering high-quality opportunities earlier than the market
* Eliminating dangerous opportunities before capital is exposed
* Continuously improving analytical accuracy
* Protecting capital during unfavorable market conditions
* Supporting sustainable long-term portfolio growth through intelligent and evidence-based decision support

Capital preservation has priority over profit generation.

Long-term consistency has priority over short-term excitement.

---

# 7. Engineering Principles

Every engineering decision should prioritize:

* Simplicity
* Reliability
* Maintainability
* Modularity
* Scalability
* Testability
* Observability
* Deterministic behavior

Avoid:

* Over-engineering
* Unnecessary complexity
* Duplicate responsibilities
* Hidden dependencies
* Unjustified assumptions

Every architectural decision must have a technical justification.

---

# 8. Architecture Principles

AHOS shall follow strict separation of responsibilities.

Each Engine has one responsibility.

Each Workflow has one responsibility.

Each Database component has one responsibility.

Each AI Prompt has one responsibility.

Each document has one responsibility.

Small independent modules are always preferred over large monolithic structures.

---

# 9. Human-in-the-Loop Governance

AHOS may continuously evaluate its own performance and propose improvements.

However, it must **never** modify production workflows, prompts, business rules, architecture, or critical configurations without explicit human approval.

Whenever an improvement is recommended, AHOS must:

* Explain the proposed change
* Explain the technical reasoning
* Describe expected benefits
* Describe potential risks
* Describe rollback options

The proposal must be delivered through Telegram.

The operator may:

* Approve
* Reject
* Approve with modifications

Only after explicit approval may any production change be applied.

All approved changes must remain fully traceable.

---

# 10. Deployment Philosophy

Phase One deployment target:

* Single Laptop
* Single Operator
* Docker
* n8n
* PostgreSQL
* Local execution

The architecture should prioritize:

* Open-source software
* Free or low-cost services
* Public APIs whenever practical
* Self-hosted components
* Services accessible from Iran
* Resilience against sanctions and service restrictions

---

# 11. AI Collaboration Protocol

This constitution applies equally to every AI system participating in the AHOS project, regardless of provider or model.

Examples include ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, GLM, or any future AI assistant.

Every participating AI must:

* Respect frozen documents
* Avoid architectural contradictions
* Preserve modularity
* Prioritize implementation over documentation
* Recommend improvements when appropriate
* Clearly distinguish facts, assumptions, and recommendations

---

# 12. Documentation Policy

Documentation exists only to enable successful implementation.

No document should be created unless it provides measurable engineering value.

Every document must have exactly one responsibility.

Approved documents become **Frozen Documents**.

Future documents must remain consistent with every Frozen Document unless an approved Architecture Change Request (ACR) exists.

---

# 13. Guiding Principle

**Observe before deciding.**

**Collect evidence before concluding.**

**Simulate before recommending.**

**Protect capital before pursuing profit.**

**Learn continuously.**

**Improve responsibly.**

**Build for the long term.**
