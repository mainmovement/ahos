# AHOS System Vision & Functional Objectives

### Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose of This Document

This document defines **what AHOS must be capable of doing**.

It does **not** define software architecture, implementation details, workflows, database structures, or source code.

Its purpose is to define the complete functional vision of the system.

Every future engineering document must satisfy the functional objectives defined here.

---

# 2. Functional Vision

AHOS is designed to become an autonomous intelligence platform specialized in discovering, evaluating, simulating, ranking, and continuously monitoring **early-stage decentralized token opportunities**.

The platform must continuously evolve its analytical capabilities and improve its understanding of the market through evidence collection, simulation, learning, and performance evaluation.

Its purpose is not simply to generate trading signals.

Its purpose is to become a professional intelligence system capable of supporting high-quality trading decisions.

---

# 3. Primary Functional Objective

The primary objective of AHOS is to discover decentralized token opportunities **before they become widely recognized by the market**.

Every subsystem should contribute toward improving:

* Early discovery
* Evidence quality
* Risk evaluation
* Opportunity ranking
* Decision support
* Learning capability
* Portfolio growth

---

# 4. Operational Scope

AHOS focuses primarily on decentralized tokens launched or discovered through ecosystems such as:

* DEXTools
* DexScreener
* GeckoTerminal
* Birdeye
* Pump.fun
* Moonshot
* Raydium LaunchLab
* Uniswap Ecosystem
* PancakeSwap Ecosystem
* Base Ecosystem
* Solana Ecosystem
* Ethereum Ecosystem
* BNB Chain Ecosystem
* Future decentralized launch platforms

The system is not intended to operate as a general cryptocurrency analysis platform.

Large-cap cryptocurrencies may only be monitored when they improve contextual understanding for decentralized token opportunities.

---

# 5. Functional Objectives

AHOS should be capable of continuously performing the following major functions:

## 5.1 Market Intelligence

Continuously collect and normalize information from multiple independent sources.

## 5.2 Opportunity Discovery

Identify new token opportunities as early as possible.

## 5.3 Security Evaluation

Evaluate contracts, developers, liquidity, ownership, and other risk indicators before any recommendation.

## 5.4 Social Intelligence

Monitor news, social media, community growth, influencer activity, and sentiment.

## 5.5 On-Chain Intelligence

Analyze wallet behavior, whale activity, smart-money movements, liquidity events, and blockchain transactions.

## 5.6 Opportunity Scoring

Assign transparent and explainable scores to every opportunity using multiple evidence categories.

## 5.7 Simulation

Evaluate candidate opportunities using extensive scenario simulations before generating recommendations.

## 5.8 Learning

Learn continuously from historical outcomes, simulation accuracy, and market behavior.

## 5.9 Portfolio Intelligence

Evaluate every recommendation within the context of the current portfolio, available capital, and overall portfolio risk.

## 5.10 Decision Support

Generate clear, evidence-based recommendations for the operator rather than making autonomous trading decisions.

---

# 6. Functional Philosophy

Every recommendation generated by AHOS must satisfy four fundamental questions:

1. Why is this opportunity interesting?

2. What evidence supports it?

3. What risks have been identified?

4. Why is this opportunity better than competing alternatives?

If these questions cannot be answered with sufficient confidence, AHOS should not recommend the opportunity.
