# AHOS System Objectives

### Version 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic objectives that AHOS must achieve throughout its lifecycle.

These objectives define **what the platform is expected to accomplish**, not **how it should be implemented**.

Every future architecture, workflow, AI model, database, prompt, and implementation decision must support one or more of these objectives.

---

# 2. Primary Objective

The primary objective of AHOS is to become an autonomous intelligence platform capable of discovering exceptional decentralized token opportunities before they become widely recognized by the market.

The platform shall continuously improve its ability to identify high-quality opportunities while reducing uncertainty, avoiding unnecessary risks, and supporting intelligent human decision making.

# 7. Human Governance Objectives

The platform shall never independently modify production components.

Every proposed improvement must first be submitted to the operator.

Only after explicit approval may any production modification be implemented.

Every approved modification shall remain completely traceable.
