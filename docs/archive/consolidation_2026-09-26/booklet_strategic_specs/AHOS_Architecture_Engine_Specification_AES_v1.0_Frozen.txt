# AHOS Engine Specification (AES)

### Version 1.0

### Frozen Engineering Document

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines every intelligence engine within AHOS.

Each engine has one and only one responsibility.

No engine may perform responsibilities assigned to another engine.

All communication between engines must occur through clearly defined interfaces.

---

# 2. Engine Design Principles

Every engine shall be:

• Independent

• Modular

• Replaceable

• Testable

• Observable

• Scalable

• Fault Tolerant

• Version Controlled

Every engine must have:

• Defined Inputs

• Defined Outputs

• Defined Responsibilities

• Defined Dependencies

• Defined Performance Metrics

# 21. Future Engine Expansion

AHOS architecture shall allow unlimited future engines.

New engines shall not require redesign of existing engines.

Every future engine must follow this document.

---

# 22. Engine Communication Rules

Engines communicate only through defined interfaces.

No engine may directly modify another engine.

No engine may bypass another engine.

All communication shall be logged.

All failures shall be traceable.

---

# 23. Engine Responsibility Rule

Every engine has exactly one primary responsibility.

If an engine grows beyond its responsibility,

it must be divided into multiple independent engines.

No engine shall become a monolith.

---

# 24. Engineering Philosophy

Small engines.

Small workflows.

Small prompts.

Small databases.

Small responsibilities.

Together they create one intelligent platform.

This philosophy shall govern every future engineering decision within AHOS.
