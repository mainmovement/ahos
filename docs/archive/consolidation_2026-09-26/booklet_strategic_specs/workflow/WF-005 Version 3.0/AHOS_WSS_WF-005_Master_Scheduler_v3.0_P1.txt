AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-005 — Data Acquisition Manager

Version 1.0

Engine: Data Acquisition Engine

Phase 1 — Data Acquisition Architecture & Core Principles
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL serve as the authoritative architectural component responsible for acquiring, validating, coordinating, and supervising external market data entering the AHOS platform.

It SHALL establish the trusted boundary between external information sources and the internal architecture.

1.2 Scope

WF-005 governs:

External Data Acquisition
Source Coordination
Data Collection Lifecycle
Acquisition Scheduling Requests
Source Availability Verification
Acquisition Integrity
Data Origin Traceability
Collection Supervision

WF-005 SHALL NOT perform data normalization, feature extraction, market analysis, or trading decisions.

2. Architectural Responsibility

WF-005 SHALL be the single authoritative owner of external data acquisition.

No other workflow SHALL independently collect production market data unless explicitly authorized by architectural policy.

3. Architectural Position
External Sources
        │
        ▼
WF-005 Data Acquisition Manager
        │
        ▼
Data Quality Engine
        │
        ▼
Normalization Engine

WF-005 SHALL represent the only architectural gateway through which external operational data enters AHOS.

4. Acquisition Philosophy

External information SHALL be considered untrusted until successfully acquired, validated, and accepted according to architectural policy.

Successful retrieval of data SHALL NOT imply that the data is trustworthy.

5. Core Principles

Data acquisition SHALL remain:

deterministic;
traceable;
source-aware;
fault-tolerant;
auditable;
technology-independent;
scalable.

Every acquisition SHALL be independently reproducible.

6. Source Independence

WF-005 SHALL remain independent from specific providers.

Supported providers MAY include:

DexScreener
DEXTools
GeckoTerminal
CoinGecko
CoinMarketCap
Binance
Hyperliquid
Future Providers

The architectural behavior SHALL remain unchanged regardless of the provider.

7. Trust Boundary

WF-005 SHALL establish the official trust boundary between:

External Systems
Internal AHOS Components

All downstream components SHALL consume only data that has successfully crossed this boundary.

8. Data Ownership

Before acceptance, external information SHALL remain owned by the originating provider.

After successful acquisition and acceptance, AHOS SHALL become the authoritative owner of the internal acquisition record while preserving source attribution.

9. Acquisition Constraints

WF-005 SHALL NOT:

normalize data;
enrich data;
score assets;
evaluate opportunities;
execute trading logic;
modify external provider data.

Its sole responsibility SHALL remain reliable acquisition and controlled admission of external information.

10. Architectural Assumptions

WF-005 assumes:

external providers may become unavailable;
data formats may differ;
response latency may vary;
rate limits may exist;
partial responses may occur;
network failures are expected.

The architecture SHALL tolerate these conditions without compromising platform integrity.

11. Phase 1 Completion Statement

Phase 1 establishes the architectural foundation of the AHOS Data Acquisition Manager.

It defines the role, authority, trust boundary, scope, and operational principles governing all external data entering the AHOS platform.

This phase intentionally separates data acquisition from all downstream processing activities, ensuring that the acquisition layer remains focused solely on reliable, deterministic, and auditable ingestion of external information.
