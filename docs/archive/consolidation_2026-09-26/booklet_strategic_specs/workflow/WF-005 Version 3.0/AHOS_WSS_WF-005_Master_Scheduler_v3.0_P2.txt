AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-005 — Data Acquisition Manager
Version 1.0
Phase 2 — Source Management, Acquisition Lifecycle & Trust Establishment
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural framework governing external data source management, acquisition lifecycle, source registration, trust establishment, and acquisition coordination within the AHOS platform.

Every external data acquisition SHALL follow a deterministic lifecycle before information becomes eligible for downstream processing.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Data Source Management
Source Registration
Source Classification
Acquisition Lifecycle
Trust Establishment
Source Capability Assessment
Acquisition Coordination
Source Availability Tracking

Data quality validation remains outside the scope of this phase.

2. Source Management Philosophy

External data sources SHALL be treated as independent providers with varying capabilities, reliability, latency, and operational characteristics.

The architecture SHALL adapt to heterogeneous providers without modifying acquisition principles.

3. Source Management Principles

Source management SHALL remain:

provider-independent;
deterministic;
extensible;
traceable;
auditable;
policy-driven.

Adding or removing a provider SHALL NOT require architectural redesign.

4. Source Registration

Every external provider SHALL be registered before participating in production data acquisition.

Each source registration SHALL define:

Source Identifier
Source Name
Source Category
Supported Data Types
Authentication Requirements
Rate Limit Policy
Availability Expectations
Version Information

Unregistered sources SHALL NOT participate in production acquisition.

5. Source Classification

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL classify external providers according to their architectural role.

Minimum categories SHALL include:

Market Data Providers

Examples:

DexScreener
GeckoTerminal
CoinGecko
Exchange Providers

Examples:

Binance
Hyperliquid
Analytics Providers

Examples:

DEXTools
On-chain analytics platforms

Additional categories MAY be introduced without architectural modification.

6. Source Capability Model

Each registered source SHALL declare its capabilities, including:

Supported Networks
Supported Assets
Available Endpoints
Update Frequency
Historical Data Availability
Real-Time Data Support

Capability declarations SHALL remain version-controlled.

7. Acquisition Lifecycle

Every acquisition SHALL progress through the following lifecycle:

Source Selected
        │
        ▼
Availability Verified
        │
        ▼
Acquisition Requested
        │
        ▼
Response Received
        │
        ▼
Integrity Checked
        │
        ▼
Trust Boundary Passed
        │
        ▼
Accepted for Downstream Processing

No acquisition SHALL bypass lifecycle stages.

8. Trust Establishment

Before crossing the architectural trust boundary, every acquisition SHALL satisfy:

source registration validation;
successful communication;
response completeness verification;
protocol compliance;
acquisition integrity checks.

Trust establishment SHALL apply to the acquisition process, not to the semantic correctness of the data.

9. Source Availability

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL continuously maintain availability information for every registered provider.

Availability SHALL include:

Reachability
Response Status
Recent Success Ratio
Communication Latency
Last Successful Acquisition

Availability SHALL NOT imply data quality.

10. Acquisition Coordination

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL coordinate acquisition requests while respecting:

provider-specific rate limits;
provider capabilities;
architectural priorities;
operational availability;
retry policies defined elsewhere.

Coordination SHALL remain deterministic.

11. Source Metadata

For every successful acquisition, the following metadata SHALL be preserved:

Source Identifier
Acquisition Identifier
Provider Version
Endpoint Identifier
Acquisition Timestamp
Correlation Identifier

Source metadata SHALL remain immutable after acquisition.

12. Architectural Constraints

WF-005 SHALL NOT:

normalize acquired data;
remove inconsistencies;
infer missing values;
evaluate market conditions;
calculate trading signals.

Its responsibility SHALL end once trusted acquisition has been completed and the data has been admitted into the AHOS processing pipeline.

13. Lifecycle Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Registration Invariant

Every production acquisition SHALL originate from a registered source.

Trust Invariant

Every accepted acquisition SHALL successfully cross the defined trust boundary.

Metadata Invariant

Every acquisition SHALL preserve complete source metadata.

Capability Invariant

Source capabilities SHALL be explicitly declared before operational use.

Independence Invariant

Provider-specific implementation details SHALL NOT alter architectural behavior.

15. Phase 2 Completion Statement

Phase 2 establishes the architectural framework for managing external data sources within AHOS.

It defines how providers are registered, classified, evaluated, coordinated, and admitted through a controlled acquisition lifecycle while preserving source traceability, operational consistency, and architectural independence.

By introducing a formal source management and trust establishment model, this phase ensures that all downstream engines receive data only after it has passed through a deterministic and auditable acquisition process.
