AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-005 — Data Acquisition Manager
Version 1.0
Phase 4 — Data Freshness, Acquisition Reliability & Quality Assurance
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural framework governing acquisition freshness, acquisition reliability, completeness verification, and operational quality assurance for all externally acquired data.

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL ensure that only operationally valid acquisition results proceed to downstream processing.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Data Freshness
Acquisition Reliability
Acquisition Completeness
Source Responsiveness
Quality Assurance
Acquisition Validity
Reliability Monitoring
Freshness Evaluation

Semantic data validation remains outside the scope of this specification.

2. Quality Philosophy

Successful data retrieval SHALL NOT imply acceptable acquisition quality.

Every acquisition SHALL satisfy minimum operational quality requirements before being admitted to the AHOS processing pipeline.

3. Quality Principles

Acquisition quality SHALL remain:

deterministic;
measurable;
provider-independent;
reproducible;
auditable;
policy-driven.

Operational quality SHALL be evaluated independently from business value.

4. Data Freshness

Every acquisition SHALL include an explicitly defined freshness characteristic.

Freshness evaluation SHALL consider:

Acquisition Timestamp
Source Update Timestamp
Expected Update Frequency
Acquisition Delay
Freshness Policy

Freshness SHALL be evaluated before downstream admission.

5. Freshness Categories

The architecture SHALL recognize the following freshness states:

Current
Acceptable
Aging
Stale
Expired

Freshness thresholds SHALL remain configurable through architectural policy.

6. Acquisition Reliability

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL continuously evaluate provider reliability using objective operational evidence.

Reliability assessment MAY include:

Success Ratio
Response Consistency
Timeout Frequency
Retry Frequency
Communication Stability

Reliability SHALL be maintained independently for each provider.

7. Acquisition Completeness

Every acquisition SHALL be evaluated for operational completeness.

Completeness assessment SHALL verify:

Expected response structure
Mandatory metadata availability
Required payload presence
Acquisition integrity
Response termination status

Incomplete acquisitions SHALL NOT automatically be accepted.

8. Acquisition Validity

Operational validity SHALL require:

successful provider communication;
complete acquisition lifecycle;
freshness compliance;
completeness verification;
integrity preservation.

Validity SHALL refer only to the acquisition process.

Business correctness SHALL be evaluated by downstream engines.

9. Reliability History

Provider reliability history SHALL remain permanently available for:

provider comparison;
operational analytics;
provider prioritization;
incident investigation;
future optimization.

Historical reliability SHALL NEVER be overwritten.

10. Source Performance Observation

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL preserve operational statistics including:

Average Response Time
Success Ratio
Failure Ratio
Availability Ratio
Freshness Compliance
Acquisition Throughput

Performance observation SHALL remain provider-specific.

11. Quality Assurance

Every accepted acquisition SHALL preserve evidence demonstrating that operational quality requirements were satisfied.

Quality evidence SHALL remain immutable.

12. Quality Audit

Every acquisition quality assessment SHALL generate immutable audit information including:

Acquisition Identifier
Provider Identifier
Freshness State
Reliability Result
Completeness Result
Quality Outcome
Assessment Timestamp
Correlation Identifier

Audit information SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

13. Quality Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Freshness Invariant

Every accepted acquisition SHALL possess a valid freshness classification.

Reliability Invariant

Provider reliability SHALL be continuously measurable.

Completeness Invariant

Every accepted acquisition SHALL satisfy mandatory completeness requirements.

Validity Invariant

Operational validity SHALL be evaluated before downstream admission.

Audit Invariant

Every quality decision SHALL remain fully traceable.

14. Phase 4 Compliance Matrix
Requirement	Mandatory
Freshness Evaluation	SHALL
Reliability Assessment	SHALL
Completeness Verification	SHALL
Acquisition Validity	SHALL
Provider Performance Observation	SHALL
Quality Audit	SHALL
Historical Preservation	SHALL

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL invalidate Phase 4 compliance.

15. Phase 4 Completion Statement

Phase 4 establishes the acquisition quality architecture of the AHOS Data Acquisition Manager.

It defines how freshness, reliability, completeness, and operational validity are evaluated to ensure that only acquisition results meeting defined architectural quality requirements are admitted into the AHOS processing pipeline.

By separating acquisition quality assurance from semantic data validation, this phase enables downstream engines to focus on interpretation and intelligence while relying on a trusted and operationally consistent acquisition layer.
