AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-005 — Data Acquisition Manager
Version 1.0
Phase 8 — Data Admission, Handover Contracts & Downstream Readiness
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural framework governing controlled data admission, downstream handover, readiness verification, and contractual transfer of acquired data from the Data Acquisition Manager to subsequent AHOS processing engines.

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL ensure that only acquisition results satisfying all architectural admission requirements are released for downstream processing.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Data Admission
Downstream Handover
Admission Contracts
Readiness Verification
Transfer Integrity
Release Coordination
Processing Eligibility
Downstream Traceability

Downstream processing logic remains outside the scope of this specification.

2. Admission Philosophy

Acquisition completion SHALL NOT automatically authorize downstream processing.

Every acquisition SHALL explicitly satisfy admission requirements before release.

3. Admission Principles

Data admission SHALL remain:

deterministic;
explicit;
auditable;
traceable;
technology-independent;
contract-driven.

Implicit downstream admission SHALL NOT occur.

4. Admission Requirements

Before release, every acquisition SHALL satisfy:

successful acquisition lifecycle;
source registration verification;
operational quality requirements;
identity assignment;
provenance preservation;
correlation continuity;
audit readiness.

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL prevent downstream admission.

5. Handover Contract

The Data Acquisition Manager SHALL transfer data using a formal architectural contract.

Every admitted acquisition SHALL include:

Acquisition Identifier
Correlation Identifier
Source Provenance
Acquisition Metadata
Freshness Classification
Reliability Status
Admission Timestamp
Admission Version

The contract SHALL remain stable across implementation technologies.

6. Downstream Readiness

WF-005 SHALL guarantee that admitted acquisitions are operationally ready for:

WF-006 Data Quality Engine
Future Processing Engines
Audit Services
Historical Replay
Analytics

WF-005 SHALL NOT guarantee semantic correctness.

7. Admission Outcome

Every acquisition SHALL receive exactly one admission outcome:

Admitted
Rejected
Deferred

No acquisition SHALL exist without an explicit admission decision.

8. Transfer Integrity

The architectural handover SHALL preserve:

identity;
provenance;
timestamps;
correlation;
audit references.

Downstream workflows SHALL receive identical admission information.

9. Downstream Independence

WF-006 and subsequent workflows SHALL consume admitted data without requiring knowledge of internal acquisition procedures.

Implementation details SHALL remain encapsulated within WF-005.

10. Admission Audit

Every admission decision SHALL generate immutable audit evidence including:

Admission Identifier
Acquisition Identifier
Admission Outcome
Decision Timestamp
Admission Version
Correlation Identifier

Admission history SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

11. Admission Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Admission Invariant

Every downstream acquisition SHALL possess an explicit admission decision.

Contract Invariant

Every admitted acquisition SHALL satisfy the architectural handover contract.

Identity Invariant

Identity SHALL remain unchanged throughout handover.

Traceability Invariant

Admission decisions SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

Independence Invariant

Downstream engines SHALL remain independent from acquisition implementation details.

13. Phase 8 Completion Statement

Phase 8 establishes the admission and handover architecture of the AHOS Data Acquisition Manager.

It defines how externally acquired information is formally admitted into the AHOS processing pipeline through deterministic admission policies and stable architectural contracts.

By separating acquisition from downstream processing through explicit admission decisions, this phase ensures that every subsequent engine operates upon trusted, traceable, and contract-compliant acquisition results.
