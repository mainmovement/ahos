AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-004 — Health Monitor
Version 1.0
Phase 7 — Operational Health Authority & Health Information Distribution
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural authority governing health information publication, operational health distribution, authoritative health consumption, and platform-wide health consistency.

The Health Monitor SHALL serve as the single authoritative provider of operational health information for the AHOS platform.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Health Authority
Health Publication
Health Distribution
Health Consumers
Health Consistency
Health Synchronization
Operational Visibility
Health Information Integrity

Operational decision-making remains outside the scope of this specification.

2. Authority Philosophy

Health information SHALL originate from exactly one authoritative architectural component.

Multiple independent sources of platform health SHALL NOT exist.

The Health Monitor SHALL remain the canonical source of operational health.

3. Authority Principles

Health authority SHALL remain:

centralized;
deterministic;
consistent;
reproducible;
auditable;
implementation-independent.

Authoritative health SHALL NEVER depend upon consumer interpretation.

4. Authoritative Health Model

WF-004 SHALL maintain the authoritative representation of:

Platform Health
Runtime Health
Component Health
Dependency Health
Operational Readiness

No workflow SHALL redefine these values independently.

5. Health Consumers

The Health Monitor SHALL expose health information for authorized architectural consumers including:

WF-001 Master Scheduler
WF-003 Execution Manager
WF-023 Operator Control Center
WF-024 Notification Engine
WF-025 Audit Trail
Future Runtime Components

Consumers SHALL treat published health as authoritative.

6. Health Distribution

Health information SHALL be distributed using implementation-independent mechanisms.

Distribution SHALL preserve:

consistency;
chronology;
integrity;
correlation;
traceability.

Distribution technology SHALL remain outside the scope of this specification.

7. Information Consistency

Every consumer requesting health information at the same logical point in time SHALL receive equivalent authoritative health information.

Health inconsistencies between consumers SHALL NOT occur.

8. Publication Integrity

Published health information SHALL preserve:

Health Identifier
Assessment Identifier
Current Health State
Assessment Timestamp
Version Identifier
Correlation Identifier

Published information SHALL remain immutable until superseded by a newer authoritative assessment.

9. Health Synchronization

The Health Monitor SHALL ensure that health publications remain synchronized across all authorized consumers.

Delayed delivery SHALL NOT alter the authoritative health state.

Consumers MAY temporarily lag behind publication but SHALL NOT redefine health.

10. Information Lifetime

Every published health record SHALL remain valid until:

superseded by a newer assessment;
explicitly invalidated by architectural policy.

Expired health SHALL NOT remain authoritative.

11. Consumer Independence

Health consumers SHALL remain independent of internal Health Monitor implementation.

Consumers SHALL interact only through published architectural contracts.

Implementation changes within WF-004 SHALL NOT require consumer redesign.

12. Authority Audit

Every authoritative publication SHALL generate immutable audit information including:

Publication Identifier
Health Identifier
Publication Timestamp
Published State
Version Identifier
Correlation Identifier

Publication history SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

13. Authority Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Authority Invariant

Exactly one authoritative health representation SHALL exist.

Consistency Invariant

All consumers SHALL observe equivalent authoritative health.

Synchronization Invariant

Health publication SHALL preserve consistency across the platform.

Independence Invariant

Consumers SHALL remain implementation-independent.

Audit Invariant

Every authoritative publication SHALL remain permanently traceable.

14. Phase 7 Compliance Matrix
Requirement	Mandatory
Single Health Authority	SHALL
Consistent Health Distribution	SHALL
Consumer Independence	SHALL
Health Synchronization	SHALL
Immutable Publication	SHALL
Authority Audit	SHALL
Operational Consistency	SHALL
Technology Independence	SHALL

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL invalidate Phase 7 compliance.

15. Phase 7 Completion Statement

Phase 7 establishes the authoritative health information architecture of the AHOS platform.

It defines how health information is published, distributed, synchronized, and consumed while preserving consistency, traceability, and architectural independence.

By establishing WF-004 as the single source of operational health truth, this phase eliminates conflicting health interpretations and enables reliable coordination across all Runtime Engine components.
