AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-004 — Health Monitor
Version 1.0
Phase 8 — Operational Analytics, Health Governance & Observability
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural framework governing operational analytics, health governance, historical trend analysis, health observability, and continuous operational insight across the AHOS platform.

The Health Monitor SHALL provide authoritative operational intelligence while remaining independent from operational decision-making.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Health Analytics
Operational Statistics
Historical Trend Analysis
Health KPIs
Health Governance
Operational Reporting
Observability Integration
Continuous Improvement Readiness

Operational remediation remains outside the scope of this specification.

2. Governance Philosophy

Health information SHALL remain useful beyond immediate operational status.

Historical health evidence SHALL support long-term architectural improvement.

Operational analytics SHALL never modify operational health.

3. Governance Principles

Health governance SHALL remain:

deterministic;
evidence-based;
reproducible;
auditable;
implementation-independent;
non-intrusive.

Analytics SHALL observe operations without influencing them.

4. Operational Analytics

The Health Monitor SHALL maintain operational analytics including:

Health State Distribution
Component Availability
Health Transition Frequency
Assessment Success Ratio
Assessment Failure Ratio
Monitoring Coverage
Dependency Failure Frequency
Runtime Readiness History

Additional analytics MAY be introduced without architectural modification.

5. Historical Trend Analysis

Historical health information SHALL support:

operational trend analysis;
recurring failure identification;
infrastructure capacity planning;
dependency stability analysis;
architectural optimization.

Historical analysis SHALL preserve chronological integrity.

6. Health KPIs

The Health Monitor SHALL calculate health-related KPIs including:

Platform Availability
Component Availability
Mean Time Between Health Failures (MTBHF)
Mean Time to Health Recovery (MTHR)
Health Assessment Success Rate
Monitoring Continuity
Runtime Readiness Ratio
Dependency Reliability

KPI definitions SHALL remain version-controlled.

7. Observability Integration

The Health Monitor SHALL expose standardized operational information suitable for integration with:

dashboards;
reporting systems;
audit systems;
metrics platforms;
operational analytics services.

Integration mechanisms SHALL remain implementation-specific.

8. Governance Reporting

The Health Monitor SHALL maintain reporting readiness for:

operational reviews;
incident investigations;
architectural audits;
capacity reviews;
platform governance.

Reports SHALL be reproducible using historical evidence.

9. Health History Preservation

Health history SHALL remain:

immutable;
chronological;
complete;
reconstructable;
independently verifiable.

Historical records SHALL NEVER be modified after publication.

10. Continuous Improvement Readiness

Operational analytics SHALL support future improvements including:

threshold optimization;
monitoring refinement;
dependency optimization;
infrastructure planning;
operational policy evolution.

Improvement recommendations SHALL remain external to WF-004.

11. Architectural Independence

WF-004 SHALL provide operational insight.

WF-004 SHALL NOT:

modify monitoring policies;
optimize infrastructure;
trigger workflow execution;
perform automatic recovery;
change architectural configuration.

Its responsibility SHALL remain observation, analysis, and authoritative reporting.

12. Operational Audit

Every operational analytics publication SHALL generate immutable audit information including:

Analytics Identifier
Reporting Timestamp
Covered Time Period
KPI Version
Health Dataset Reference
Correlation Identifier

Operational evidence SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

13. Governance Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Analytics Invariant

Operational analytics SHALL be derived solely from authoritative health evidence.

Historical Invariant

Health history SHALL remain immutable.

KPI Invariant

Equivalent health evidence SHALL always produce identical KPI values.

Independence Invariant

Operational analytics SHALL NEVER alter operational behavior.

Governance Invariant

Every published operational report SHALL remain fully traceable to its underlying health evidence.

14. Phase 8 Compliance Matrix
Requirement	Mandatory
Operational Analytics	SHALL
Historical Trend Analysis	SHALL
Health KPIs	SHALL
Governance Reporting	SHALL
Observability Integration	SHALL
Immutable Health History	SHALL
Operational Audit	SHALL

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL invalidate Phase 8 compliance.

15. Phase 8 Completion Statement

Phase 8 establishes the operational analytics and governance architecture of the AHOS Health Monitor.

It defines how health information is transformed into long-term operational insight while preserving deterministic behavior, historical integrity, and architectural independence.

By separating health assessment from operational analytics and governance, this phase enables continuous improvement, enterprise reporting, capacity planning, and architectural optimization without compromising the integrity of runtime monitoring.
