AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-004 — Health Monitor
Version 1.0
Phase 3 — Health Evidence, Metrics & Assessment Rules
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural framework governing health evidence collection, metric evaluation, rule-based assessment, and evidence integrity across the AHOS platform.

Every health decision SHALL be supported by objective operational evidence.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Health Evidence
Health Metrics
Assessment Rules
Rule Evaluation
Metric Validation
Evidence Aggregation
Assessment Consistency
Health Confidence

Alert generation remains outside the scope of this phase.

2. Evidence Philosophy

Health SHALL be evidence-driven.

No health classification SHALL rely upon assumptions.

Every health conclusion SHALL be reproducible using collected evidence.

3. Evidence Principles

Health evidence SHALL remain:

objective;
deterministic;
measurable;
traceable;
immutable;
independently verifiable.

Subjective interpretation SHALL NOT influence health assessment.

4. Health Evidence Model

Every health assessment SHALL be supported by one or more evidence records.

Each evidence record SHALL include:

Evidence Identifier
Component Identifier
Evidence Type
Observation Timestamp
Measured Value
Expected Range
Assessment Result
Correlation Identifier

Evidence SHALL remain immutable after collection.

5. Health Metric Categories

The Health Monitor SHALL evaluate metrics from, at minimum, the following categories:

Runtime Metrics
Workflow execution availability
Queue responsiveness
Execution latency
Scheduler responsiveness
Infrastructure Metrics
CPU utilization
Memory utilization
Disk availability
Network connectivity
Database Metrics
PostgreSQL connectivity
Query latency
Transaction responsiveness
Connection pool utilization
External Dependency Metrics
API availability
API response latency
API success ratio
External timeout frequency
Resource Metrics
Queue depth
Active execution count
Worker utilization
Resource exhaustion indicators
6. Assessment Rules

Every health metric SHALL be evaluated using predefined deterministic rules.

Assessment rules SHALL define:

acceptable range;
warning threshold;
critical threshold;
evaluation frequency;
evidence requirements.

Rules SHALL remain version-controlled.

7. Rule Evaluation

Rule evaluation SHALL occur independently for each metric.

Metric failures SHALL NOT automatically invalidate unrelated metrics.

Composite health SHALL be determined only after completion of all applicable rule evaluations.

8. Evidence Aggregation

Multiple evidence records MAY contribute to a single health assessment.

Aggregation SHALL preserve:

evidence chronology;
evidence origin;
evidence integrity;
metric traceability.

Aggregation SHALL NOT modify original evidence.

9. Health Confidence

Every health assessment SHALL produce a confidence level representing the completeness and reliability of supporting evidence.

Confidence SHALL decrease when:

required evidence is unavailable;
evidence becomes outdated;
conflicting evidence exists.

Health confidence SHALL NOT replace health state.

10. Evidence Validation

Before accepting evidence, the Health Monitor SHALL verify:

evidence source validity;
timestamp consistency;
metric completeness;
measurement integrity;
duplicate prevention.

Invalid evidence SHALL be rejected.

11. Metric Consistency

Equivalent operational conditions SHALL always produce identical metric evaluations.

Metric interpretation SHALL remain deterministic.

12. Evidence History

Historical evidence SHALL remain permanently available for:

trend analysis;
incident investigation;
operational auditing;
performance comparison;
architectural improvement.

Historical evidence SHALL NEVER be overwritten.

13. Assessment Isolation

Failure of one assessment rule SHALL NOT interrupt the evaluation of unrelated metrics.

Health evaluation SHALL remain fault-tolerant.

14. Evidence Audit

Every evidence collection event SHALL generate immutable audit information including:

Evidence Identifier
Collection Timestamp
Component Identifier
Metric Identifier
Collection Outcome
Correlation Identifier

Audit information SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

15. Assessment Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Evidence Invariant

Every health state SHALL be supported by valid evidence.

Metric Invariant

Every metric SHALL be evaluated using deterministic rules.

Confidence Invariant

Every assessment SHALL expose an evidence confidence level.

Integrity Invariant

Evidence SHALL remain immutable after collection.

Traceability Invariant

Every health decision SHALL be traceable to its originating evidence.

17. Phase 3 Completion Statement

Phase 3 establishes the evidence-driven assessment architecture of the AHOS Health Monitor.

It defines how operational evidence is collected, validated, aggregated, and evaluated through deterministic rules to produce trustworthy health classifications.

By separating evidence collection from health interpretation, this phase enables future implementation of advanced analytics, predictive monitoring, anomaly detection, and intelligent operational dashboards without requiring architectural redesign.
