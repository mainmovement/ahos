AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-004 — Health Monitor

Version 1.0

Phase 1 — Health Monitoring Architecture & Core Principles

این Workflow قرار نیست صرفاً CPU و RAM را چک کند.

این بزرگ‌ترین اشتباهی است که اکثر سیستم‌ها مرتکب می‌شوند.

WF-004 باید سلامت معماری کل AHOS را پایش کند.

نه فقط سلامت سیستم‌عامل.

1. Purpose

The Health Monitor SHALL serve as the authoritative architectural component responsible for continuous assessment of the operational health, availability, and integrity of the AHOS platform.

Health monitoring SHALL provide deterministic operational visibility without interfering with workflow execution.

2. Scope

WF-004 SHALL govern:

Runtime Health Assessment
Infrastructure Health Monitoring
Workflow Operational Health
Service Availability
Database Connectivity Health
External API Availability
Scheduler Health
Execution Manager Health
Registry Health
Resource Utilization Assessment
System Readiness Evaluation

WF-004 SHALL NOT perform recovery actions.

Recovery SHALL remain governed by other Runtime components.

3. Architectural Responsibility

WF-004 SHALL be the single authoritative source of health status for the AHOS platform.

No other workflow SHALL independently define global system health.

4. Health Philosophy

Health SHALL represent architectural readiness rather than infrastructure existence.

A running service SHALL NOT necessarily be considered healthy.

A healthy component SHALL satisfy all mandatory operational conditions defined by architectural policy.

5. Core Principles

Health monitoring SHALL be:

Continuous
Passive
Deterministic
Observable
Non-intrusive
Technology Independent
Auditable

Monitoring SHALL NEVER modify execution state.

6. Architectural Position
Runtime Layer

          ▼

          │

          │

WF-004 Health Monitor
          │

Operator Control Center

WF-004 SHALL observe the Runtime Layer without participating in execution decisions.

7. Health Domains

The Health Monitor SHALL evaluate, at minimum:

Runtime Health
Scheduler Health
Registry Health
Execution Health
Database Health
Infrastructure Health
External Dependency Health
Resource Health

Additional health domains MAY be introduced through future architectural revisions.

8. Monitoring Independence

Health assessment SHALL remain independent of:

Business logic
Workflow implementation
Market data
Trading strategy
AI analysis

Health evaluation SHALL focus exclusively on operational integrity.

9. Health Authority

WF-004 SHALL publish authoritative health status.

Health information produced by WF-004 SHALL be considered the official operational status of the AHOS platform.

10. Architectural Constraints

WF-004 SHALL NOT:

execute workflows;
schedule workflows;
retry workflows;
recover failed executions;
modify runtime state;
alter workflow configuration.

Its responsibility SHALL remain observation and assessment only.

11. Phase 1 Completion Statement

Phase 1 establishes the architectural foundation of the AHOS Health Monitor.

It defines the scope, authority, philosophy, principles, and operational boundaries governing health assessment across the Runtime Engine while preserving strict separation between monitoring and execution.
