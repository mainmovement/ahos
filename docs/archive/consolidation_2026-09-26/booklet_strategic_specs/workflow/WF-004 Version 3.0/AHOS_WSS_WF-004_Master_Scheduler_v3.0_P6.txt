AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-004 — Health Monitor
Version 1.0
Phase 6 — Health State Transition, Event Publication & Operational Notification
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the architectural framework governing health state transitions, operational event publication, state change consistency, and health notification readiness.

The Health Monitor SHALL manage every health state transition as an explicit architectural event.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Health State Transition
Transition Validation
Health Event Publication
Operational Notification Readiness
State Change Audit
Transition Consistency
Event Correlation
Operational Visibility

Notification delivery mechanisms remain outside the scope of this specification.

2. Transition Philosophy

A health state SHALL change only after successful completion of an authoritative health assessment.

Every transition SHALL represent an architectural fact rather than an operational assumption.

3. Transition Principles

Health transitions SHALL remain:

deterministic;
explicit;
auditable;
chronological;
reproducible;
policy-compliant.

Implicit transitions SHALL NOT occur.

4. State Transition Model

Every health state transition SHALL follow the authoritative state model.

Unknown
   │
   ▼
Healthy
   │
 ┌─┴─────────────┐
 ▼               ▼
Degraded     Maintenance
 │               │
 └──────┬────────┘
        ▼
Unhealthy
        │
        ▼
Unavailable

Transitions outside this model SHALL NOT occur without architectural revision.

5. Transition Validation

Before accepting a new health state, the Health Monitor SHALL validate:

assessment completion;
evidence integrity;
rule evaluation consistency;
dependency consistency;
transition eligibility.

Invalid transitions SHALL be rejected.

6. Transition Publication

Every successful transition SHALL generate a Health State Event.

Each event SHALL include:

Event Identifier
Component Identifier
Previous Health State
Current Health State
Transition Timestamp
Assessment Identifier
Correlation Identifier

Health events SHALL be immutable.

7. Event Classification

Health events SHALL be classified according to their operational significance.

The minimum event categories SHALL include:

Health Established
Health Improved
Health Degraded
Health Lost
Maintenance Entered
Maintenance Completed
Health Restored

Additional categories MAY be introduced through future architectural revisions.

8. Notification Readiness

The Health Monitor SHALL expose sufficient operational information to enable notification services.

WF-004 SHALL publish health facts.

WF-024 Notification Engine SHALL determine notification policies and delivery mechanisms.

WF-004 SHALL NOT send notifications directly.

9. Duplicate Transition Prevention

Repeated publication of identical health transitions SHALL be prevented.

Equivalent consecutive transitions SHALL NOT generate duplicate operational events.

Historical state SHALL remain unchanged.

10. Transition Ordering

Health events SHALL preserve chronological ordering.

Publication order SHALL reflect actual transition order.

Out-of-order publication SHALL NOT occur.

11. Operational Visibility

Every health transition SHALL become visible to authorized architectural components.

Visibility SHALL remain independent from notification mechanisms.

12. Transition Audit

Every transition SHALL generate immutable audit evidence including:

Transition Identifier
Component Identifier
Previous State
New State
Transition Timestamp
Assessment Reference
Correlation Identifier

Audit evidence SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

13. Transition Recovery

If transition publication fails:

the health state SHALL remain committed;
publication failure SHALL be recorded;
publication MAY be retried according to implementation policy.

Health assessment SHALL NOT be repeated solely because publication failed.

14. Transition Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Explicit Transition Invariant

Every health state change SHALL result from a validated assessment.

Event Invariant

Every successful transition SHALL generate exactly one authoritative health event.

Ordering Invariant

Health events SHALL preserve chronological order.

Notification Invariant

WF-004 SHALL publish health information but SHALL NOT deliver notifications.

Audit Invariant

Every transition SHALL remain permanently traceable.

16. Phase 6 Completion Statement

Phase 6 establishes the architectural model governing health state transitions and operational event publication within the AHOS platform.

It defines how validated health changes become authoritative operational events while preserving determinism, auditability, chronological consistency, and strict separation between health assessment and notification delivery.

By treating health transitions as first-class architectural events, this phase enables future integration with alerting systems, operational dashboards, automation workflows, and governance services without modifying the core Health Monitor architecture.
