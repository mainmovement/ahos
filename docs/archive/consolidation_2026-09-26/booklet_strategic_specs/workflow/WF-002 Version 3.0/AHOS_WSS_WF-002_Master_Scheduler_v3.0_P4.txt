AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-002 — Workflow Registry
Version 1.0
Phase 4 — Registry Validation & Consistency
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the authoritative validation and consistency mechanisms governing all architectural information maintained by the Workflow Registry.

The Registry SHALL guarantee that architectural knowledge remains internally consistent, complete, deterministic, and continuously verifiable.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Registry Validation
Metadata Validation
Identity Validation
Dependency Validation
Capability Validation
Version Validation
Consistency Verification
Integrity Enforcement
Registry Invariants

Business validation remains outside the scope of WF-002.

2. Validation Philosophy

Validation SHALL protect architectural correctness.

Validation SHALL occur before architectural acceptance.

Validation SHALL NEVER depend upon runtime execution.

Architectural correctness SHALL always take precedence over operational convenience.

3. Validation Principles

Registry validation SHALL satisfy:

Determinism
Repeatability
Completeness
Traceability
Independence
Auditability
4. Validation Domains

Every registration SHALL be validated across the following domains:

Identity
Ownership
Metadata
Version
Capability
Dependency
Contract Reference
Specification Reference
Engine Association

Additional domains MAY be introduced through future architectural revisions.

5. Identity Validation
5.1 Objective

Every workflow identity SHALL be validated before registry acceptance.

5.2 Identity Rules

Validation SHALL verify:

globally unique Workflow Identifier;
immutable identity format;
naming convention compliance;
identifier uniqueness.
5.3 Validation Result

Identity validation SHALL produce exactly one result:

Passed
Failed
6. Metadata Validation

Registry metadata SHALL be validated for:

completeness;
structural correctness;
semantic correctness;
version consistency;
ownership consistency.

Missing mandatory metadata SHALL invalidate registration.

7. Version Validation

Every workflow version SHALL satisfy:

valid version syntax;
compatible contract version;
compatible engine version;
valid publication state.

Unsupported versions SHALL NOT become Active.

8. Capability Validation

Every declared capability SHALL be validated for:

uniqueness;
ownership;
naming;
version compatibility;
architectural consistency.

Capability conflicts SHALL invalidate registration.

9. Dependency Validation

Dependency validation SHALL verify:

referenced workflow existence;
referenced version existence;
dependency type validity;
compatibility;
architectural correctness.
9.1 Circular Dependency Detection

The Registry SHALL detect circular dependencies.

Circular dependency chains SHALL be rejected before registration.

9.2 Missing Dependency Detection

Undefined dependencies SHALL invalidate registration.

10. Contract Validation

Every registered workflow SHALL reference:

one Workflow Contract Specification;
one compatible contract version.

Undefined contract references SHALL NOT be accepted.

11. Specification Validation

Every registered workflow SHALL reference:

one Workflow Specification;
one Specification Version.

Specification references SHALL remain valid throughout the workflow lifecycle.

12. Registry Integrity Verification

WF-002 SHALL continuously verify:

identity uniqueness;
metadata integrity;
dependency integrity;
ownership integrity;
version integrity.

Verification SHALL remain deterministic.

13. Consistency Model

Registry consistency SHALL guarantee that all architectural information represents one coherent architectural state.

Consumers SHALL NEVER observe inconsistent registry knowledge.

14. Consistency Rules

At any moment:

exactly one authoritative owner SHALL exist;
exactly one active version SHALL exist;
exactly one workflow identity SHALL exist;
exactly one registration status SHALL exist.

Architectural ambiguity SHALL NOT occur.

15. Validation Outcomes

Every validation process SHALL terminate with one of the following outcomes:

Accepted
Rejected
Requires Architectural Review

No additional outcome SHALL exist.

16. Validation Audit

Every validation SHALL generate immutable audit evidence.

Validation records SHALL include:

Validation Identifier
Workflow Identifier
Validation Timestamp
Validation Scope
Validation Outcome
Validation Evidence
17. Validation Failure Handling

Validation failures SHALL preserve registry integrity.

Incomplete validation SHALL NEVER create partial registry entries.

Rollback SHALL restore the previous consistent architectural state.

18. Registry Invariants

The following architectural properties SHALL remain continuously true.

Identity Invariant

Every workflow SHALL possess one immutable identity.

Ownership Invariant

Every workflow SHALL possess one authoritative owner.

Version Invariant

Every active workflow SHALL possess one active version.

Capability Invariant

Every capability SHALL possess one authoritative owner.

Dependency Invariant

Every declared dependency SHALL reference valid architectural objects.

Integrity Invariant

Registry consistency SHALL remain continuously verifiable.

19. Consistency Guarantees

WF-002 SHALL guarantee:

deterministic validation;
repeatable verification;
conflict detection;
complete traceability;
architectural correctness.
20. Phase 4 Completion Statement

Phase 4 establishes the Workflow Registry as the authoritative validation authority for workflow architecture.

It guarantees that every registered workflow, capability, dependency, version, and ownership declaration remains internally consistent, continuously verifiable, and architecturally correct throughout its lifecycle.

By enforcing deterministic validation and preserving immutable architectural integrity, this phase ensures that the Workflow Registry remains the trusted source of architectural truth for the entire AHOS platform.
