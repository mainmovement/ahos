AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-002 — Workflow Registry
Version 1.0
Phase 7 — High Availability, Synchronization & Architectural Resilience
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase defines the architectural principles governing registry availability, synchronization, consistency preservation, and resilience across current and future AHOS deployment environments.

The Workflow Registry SHALL remain the authoritative architectural source regardless of deployment topology.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Registry Availability
Registry Synchronization
Registry Replication Readiness
Consistency Preservation
Failure Isolation
Recovery Coordination
Architectural Resilience
Future Distributed Compatibility

Actual distributed runtime implementation remains outside the scope of this specification.

2. Architectural Philosophy

Architectural correctness SHALL remain independent of deployment architecture.

The Workflow Registry SHALL preserve one authoritative architectural state regardless of infrastructure evolution.

Infrastructure SHALL adapt to architecture.

Architecture SHALL NOT adapt to infrastructure limitations.

3. Availability Principles

Registry availability SHALL satisfy:

deterministic behavior;
architectural consistency;
fault isolation;
graceful degradation;
recoverability.

Availability SHALL NEVER compromise architectural integrity.

4. Registry Availability Model

The Registry SHALL remain continuously available for:

architectural discovery;
metadata retrieval;
version lookup;
dependency lookup;
capability lookup;
ownership lookup.

Temporary infrastructure degradation SHALL NOT invalidate architectural knowledge.

5. Synchronization Principles

Whenever multiple registry representations exist,

their synchronization SHALL preserve:

identity consistency;
version consistency;
ownership consistency;
metadata consistency;
dependency consistency.

Synchronization SHALL NEVER modify architectural meaning.

6. Registry Synchronization Model

Synchronization SHALL operate only upon authoritative registry information.

Synchronization SHALL NOT generate new architectural knowledge.

It SHALL propagate existing authoritative knowledge.

7. Consistency Preservation

Synchronization SHALL preserve:

immutable workflow identity;
immutable publication history;
immutable audit evidence;
deterministic metadata.

Conflicting synchronization SHALL be rejected.

8. Failure Isolation

Infrastructure failures SHALL remain isolated from architectural correctness.

Temporary communication failures SHALL NOT redefine registry authority.

Registry authority SHALL remain singular.

9. Recovery Coordination

Following infrastructure recovery,

the Registry SHALL restore:

architectural consistency;
synchronization integrity;
authoritative metadata;
immutable audit history.

Recovery SHALL NEVER fabricate architectural state.

10. Replication Readiness

WF-002 SHALL remain architecturally compatible with future registry replication.

Replication SHALL preserve:

authoritative identity;
deterministic versioning;
governance policies;
immutable audit evidence.

Replication SHALL NOT introduce multiple authorities.

11. Split-Brain Prevention

The Registry SHALL prevent architectural divergence.

Simultaneous conflicting authoritative registry states SHALL NOT exist.

Whenever conflicting registry authorities are detected,

registry modifications SHALL be suspended until architectural consistency is restored.

12. Synchronization Audit

Every synchronization event SHALL generate audit evidence including:

Synchronization Identifier
Source Authority
Target Authority
Timestamp
Synchronization Outcome
Integrity Verification Result
13. Resilience Principles

Registry resilience SHALL prioritize:

correctness;
recoverability;
determinism;
auditability.

Operational performance SHALL NEVER override architectural correctness.

14. Infrastructure Independence

WF-002 SHALL remain independent from:

deployment topology;
orchestration platform;
database technology;
runtime engine;
hosting provider.

Architectural behavior SHALL remain identical regardless of implementation.

15. Future Cluster Compatibility

The Registry SHALL remain compatible with future:

clustered schedulers;
distributed runtimes;
replicated databases;
geographically separated deployments;
multi-region architectures.

Compatibility SHALL NOT require modification of architectural contracts.

16. Registry Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true.

Authority Invariant

Exactly one authoritative architectural registry SHALL exist.

Synchronization Invariant

Synchronization SHALL preserve architectural identity.

Consistency Invariant

Architectural consistency SHALL survive infrastructure failures.

Audit Invariant

Synchronization history SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

Availability Invariant

Temporary infrastructure degradation SHALL NOT invalidate architectural knowledge.

18. Phase 7 Completion Statement

Phase 7 establishes the architectural resilience model for the Workflow Registry.

It ensures that registry authority, synchronization, consistency, and architectural integrity remain preserved regardless of deployment evolution, infrastructure failures, or future scalability requirements.

By separating architectural truth from infrastructure implementation, this phase enables the Workflow Registry to remain stable, deterministic, and authoritative throughout the evolution of the AHOS platform.
