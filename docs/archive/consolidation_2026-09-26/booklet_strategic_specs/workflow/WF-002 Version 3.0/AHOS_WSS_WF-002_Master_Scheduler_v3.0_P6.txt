AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-002 — Workflow Registry
Version 1.0
Phase 6 — Security, Governance & Change Control
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the security, governance, authorization, and change control framework governing the Workflow Registry.

The Registry SHALL preserve architectural integrity by ensuring that every modification is authorized, auditable, and policy-compliant.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Registry Security
Authorization
Governance Policies
Change Management
Approval Process
Audit Compliance
Registry Protection
Administrative Operations

Operational runtime security SHALL remain outside the scope of WF-002.

2. Governance Philosophy

Architectural governance SHALL prioritize integrity over convenience.

No architectural modification SHALL bypass governance.

Registry authority SHALL remain centralized.

3. Governance Principles

Registry governance SHALL remain:

deterministic;
transparent;
policy-driven;
auditable;
technology-independent.

Governance SHALL remain independent from implementation.

4. Registry Authority

WF-002 SHALL remain the exclusive authority responsible for governing architectural workflow metadata.

No external component SHALL modify authoritative registry information directly.

5. Authorization Model

Every registry operation SHALL execute under an explicit authorization context.

Anonymous registry modifications SHALL NOT be permitted.

Authorization SHALL be evaluated before validation.

6. Authorization Levels

The Registry SHALL distinguish, at minimum, the following authorization levels:

Registry Administrator
Architecture Maintainer
Engine Owner
Workflow Owner
Read-Only Consumer
Automated System Component

Additional authorization levels MAY be introduced through future revisions.

7. Administrative Operations

The following operations SHALL require explicit authorization:

Workflow Registration
Metadata Update
Version Publication
Lifecycle Transition
Ownership Transfer
Capability Modification
Dependency Modification
Workflow Archival

Unauthorized requests SHALL be rejected before execution.

8. Ownership Governance

Every registered workflow SHALL maintain one authoritative owner.

Ownership changes SHALL require:

authorization;
audit recording;
registry approval;
consistency verification.

Implicit ownership transfer SHALL NOT occur.

9. Change Control

All architectural changes SHALL follow a governed change process.

Each change SHALL include:

Change Identifier
Request Timestamp
Requesting Authority
Affected Workflow
Change Category
Approval Status
Completion Status
10. Approval Model

Registry modifications MAY require approval according to governance policy.

Approval SHALL remain explicit.

Automatic approval SHALL NOT occur unless defined by architectural policy.

11. Policy Enforcement

The Registry SHALL enforce:

Identity Policy
Version Policy
Ownership Policy
Dependency Policy
Capability Policy
Lifecycle Policy
Security Policy

Policy evaluation SHALL precede persistence.

12. Policy Violations

Policy violations SHALL result in immediate rejection.

Partial policy application SHALL NOT occur.

Violation evidence SHALL remain permanently auditable.

13. Audit Governance

Every governance decision SHALL generate immutable audit evidence.

Audit records SHALL include:

Decision Identifier
Actor
Timestamp
Decision Type
Decision Outcome
Justification
Correlation Identifier

Audit history SHALL remain immutable.

14. Registry Protection

WF-002 SHALL protect:

workflow identities;
ownership metadata;
version metadata;
capability metadata;
dependency metadata;
registry history.

Protected information SHALL remain tamper-evident.

15. Governance Consistency

Equivalent governance requests evaluated under equivalent architectural conditions SHALL produce equivalent decisions.

Governance SHALL remain deterministic.

16. Security Boundaries

WF-002 SHALL NOT:

authenticate users;
manage runtime credentials;
store operational secrets;
authorize runtime execution.

Those responsibilities belong to dedicated security components.

WF-002 governs architectural authorization only.

17. Governance Invariants

The following SHALL remain continuously true:

Identity Governance Invariant

Workflow identity SHALL remain immutable.

Ownership Governance Invariant

Every workflow SHALL have one authoritative owner.

Policy Governance Invariant

Every registry modification SHALL satisfy all applicable policies.

Audit Governance Invariant

Every governance decision SHALL remain historically reconstructable.

Authorization Governance Invariant

Unauthorized architectural modifications SHALL NEVER succeed.

18. Governance Failure Handling

Governance failures SHALL preserve architectural integrity.

Incomplete governance operations SHALL be rolled back.

No partial governance state SHALL remain visible.

19. Phase 6 Compliance Matrix
Requirement	Mandatory
Authorization Enforcement	SHALL
Governance Policies	SHALL
Explicit Ownership	SHALL
Immutable Audit Trail	SHALL
Deterministic Decisions	SHALL
Policy Enforcement	SHALL
Change Control	SHALL
Registry Protection	SHALL

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL invalidate Phase 6 compliance.

20. Phase 6 Completion Statement

Phase 6 establishes the governance and security foundation of the Workflow Registry.

It defines how architectural authority is exercised, how registry modifications are authorized, how policy compliance is enforced, and how immutable governance records are preserved.

By separating architectural governance from operational security, this phase ensures that the Workflow Registry remains the trusted guardian of architectural integrity throughout the lifecycle of the AHOS platform.
