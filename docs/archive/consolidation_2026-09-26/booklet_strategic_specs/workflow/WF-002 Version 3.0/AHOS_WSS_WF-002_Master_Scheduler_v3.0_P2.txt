AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-002 — Workflow Registry
Version 1.0
Phase 2 — Registration Lifecycle & Version Governance
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase defines the complete architectural lifecycle governing workflow registration, activation, version evolution, suspension, deprecation, and archival within the AHOS Workflow Registry.

It establishes the authoritative rules ensuring that every registered workflow evolves through a deterministic, auditable, and governed lifecycle.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Registration Lifecycle
Workflow Activation
Workflow Suspension
Workflow Deprecation
Workflow Archival
Version Governance
Version Compatibility
Publication Rules
Lifecycle Validation
Lifecycle Auditability

Runtime execution lifecycle SHALL remain governed by WF-001 and WF-003.

2. Registration Lifecycle Principles
2.1 Architectural Philosophy

Workflow registration represents an architectural commitment rather than a deployment activity.

Registration SHALL precede any operational availability.

Execution eligibility SHALL NEVER imply successful registration.

2.2 Deterministic Lifecycle

Every workflow SHALL progress through a predefined lifecycle.

Undefined lifecycle transitions SHALL NOT occur.

Lifecycle progression SHALL remain deterministic under all operating conditions.

2.3 Lifecycle Ownership

Lifecycle progression SHALL occur only through authorized architectural operations.

Implicit lifecycle changes SHALL NOT occur.

3. Registration Lifecycle Model

Every workflow SHALL exist in exactly one lifecycle state.

The canonical lifecycle SHALL consist of:

Draft
Submitted
Registered
Validated
Active
Suspended
Deprecated
Archived

Additional implementation-specific states MAY exist internally.

Only canonical states SHALL be architecturally visible.

4. Registration Phase
4.1 Registration Preconditions

A workflow SHALL satisfy all mandatory architectural requirements before registration.

Required artifacts SHALL include:

Workflow Specification
Workflow Contract
Version Declaration
Engine Association
Ownership Declaration
Capability Declaration
Dependency Declaration
4.2 Registration Guarantees

Successful registration SHALL guarantee:

globally unique identity;
immutable workflow identifier;
authoritative registry entry;
audit record creation;
lifecycle initialization.
4.3 Registration Failure

Incomplete registrations SHALL be rejected.

Rejected workflows SHALL NOT become discoverable.

5. Validation Phase
5.1 Objective

Validation confirms architectural correctness before operational activation.

Validation SHALL verify architectural integrity.

It SHALL NOT validate business functionality.

5.2 Validation Domains

Validation SHALL include:

identity validation;
ownership validation;
version validation;
dependency validation;
capability validation;
specification validation;
contract validation.
5.3 Validation Result

Validation SHALL produce one of the following outcomes:

Passed
Failed
Requires Review

No undefined validation outcome SHALL exist.

6. Activation Phase
6.1 Objective

Activation authorizes a registered workflow to become eligible for runtime scheduling.

Activation SHALL NOT imply immediate execution.

6.2 Activation Preconditions

Before activation:

validation SHALL succeed;
ownership SHALL exist;
compatible contract SHALL exist;
compatible engine SHALL exist;
version SHALL be approved.
6.3 Activation Guarantees

Activation SHALL guarantee:

architectural discoverability;
scheduler eligibility;
version visibility;
audit preservation.
6.4 Activation Integrity

Only one active architectural version SHALL exist unless explicitly approved by governance policy.

7. Suspension Phase
7.1 Objective

Suspension temporarily removes a workflow from execution eligibility without removing architectural knowledge.

7.2 Suspension Rules

Suspended workflows SHALL:

remain registered;
remain discoverable;
remain historically traceable;
become ineligible for new execution scheduling.
7.3 Reactivation

Suspended workflows MAY return to Active following successful authorization.

Historical continuity SHALL remain preserved.

8. Deprecation Phase
8.1 Objective

Deprecation announces the planned retirement of a workflow while preserving compatibility during the transition period.

8.2 Deprecation Guarantees

Deprecated workflows SHALL:

remain identifiable;
remain auditable;
remain versioned;
clearly communicate replacement guidance when applicable.
8.3 Consumer Responsibilities

Consumers SHALL avoid adopting deprecated workflows for new integrations.

Existing integrations MAY continue during the approved transition period.

9. Archival Phase
9.1 Objective

Archival permanently retires a workflow from operational use while preserving complete architectural history.

9.2 Archival Guarantees

Archived workflows SHALL:

remain historically available;
remain immutable;
remain auditable;
remain uniquely identifiable.

Archived workflows SHALL NOT become eligible for future activation.

10. Lifecycle Transition Rules

The Registry SHALL permit only architecturally valid transitions.

The canonical lifecycle is defined as:

Draft

   ▼

Transitions outside this model SHALL be rejected.

11. Version Governance
11.1 Objective

This section defines the authoritative governance model for workflow version evolution within the AHOS Workflow Registry.

Version governance SHALL preserve architectural stability while enabling controlled evolution.

11.2 Version Authority

Every workflow version SHALL be governed exclusively by WF-002.

No external architectural component SHALL modify workflow version metadata independently.

Version authority SHALL remain centralized.

11.3 Canonical Version Structure

Every registered workflow version SHALL include:

Major Version
Minor Version
Revision
Publication Status
Effective Date
Registration Timestamp

Version identifiers SHALL remain immutable after publication.

11.4 Version Evolution Principles

Workflow evolution SHALL be:

deterministic;
reviewable;
auditable;
backward-aware;
architecture-driven.

Version progression SHALL NEVER occur implicitly.

12. Version Compatibility
12.1 Objective

Every workflow version SHALL explicitly declare its architectural compatibility.

Compatibility SHALL NEVER be inferred.

12.2 Compatibility Domains

Each workflow version SHALL declare compatibility with:

Workflow Contract Version
Engine Version
Database Schema Version
Runtime Platform Version
API Specification Version

Additional compatibility domains MAY be introduced through future architectural revisions.

12.3 Compatibility Validation

Before activation,

WF-002 SHALL validate declared compatibility.

Incompatible versions SHALL NOT become Active.

12.4 Compatibility Integrity

Compatibility information SHALL remain immutable for published versions.

Changes SHALL require publication of a new workflow version.

13. Version Publication
13.1 Objective

Publication authorizes a validated workflow version to become an official architectural artifact.

Publication SHALL precede activation.

13.2 Publication Preconditions

Before publication:

architectural review SHALL be completed;
validation SHALL succeed;
ownership SHALL be confirmed;
specifications SHALL exist;
contracts SHALL exist.
13.3 Publication Guarantees

Publication SHALL guarantee:

immutable version identity;
permanent architectural traceability;
registry discoverability;
audit preservation.
13.4 Publication Integrity

Published workflow versions SHALL NEVER be modified.

Subsequent corrections SHALL require publication of a new version.

14. Version Retirement
14.1 Objective

Version retirement governs the controlled withdrawal of obsolete workflow versions.

Retirement SHALL preserve historical integrity.

14.2 Retirement Rules

Retired versions SHALL:

remain identifiable;
remain auditable;
remain historically reconstructable.

Retired versions SHALL NOT become eligible for future activation.

14.3 Historical Preservation

Retirement SHALL NEVER remove architectural history.

Historical references SHALL remain valid indefinitely.

15. Lifecycle Transition Governance
15.1 Objective

Lifecycle transitions SHALL occur only through explicitly authorized architectural operations.

Implicit transitions SHALL NOT exist.

15.2 Authorized Transition Rules

The following transitions SHALL be permitted:

Draft → Submitted
Submitted → Registered
Registered → Validated
Validated → Active
Active → Suspended
Suspended → Active
Active → Deprecated
Deprecated → Archived

Every other transition SHALL require explicit architectural approval.

15.3 Transition Validation

Before every lifecycle transition,

WF-002 SHALL validate:

current state;
target state;
ownership;
compatibility;
architectural constraints.
15.4 Transition Audit

Every lifecycle transition SHALL generate immutable audit evidence.

Audit records SHALL include transition rationale.

16. Lifecycle Invariants
16.1 Objective

Lifecycle Invariants define architectural truths that SHALL remain valid regardless of workflow evolution.

16.2 Identity Invariant

Workflow identity SHALL remain immutable throughout the entire lifecycle.

16.3 Ownership Invariant

Every workflow SHALL maintain exactly one authoritative owner.

16.4 Version Invariant

Exactly one workflow version SHALL be architecturally Active unless explicitly authorized otherwise.

16.5 Traceability Invariant

Every lifecycle transition SHALL remain permanently reconstructable.

16.6 Audit Invariant

Every architectural decision SHALL generate immutable historical evidence.

17. Lifecycle Failure Handling
17.1 Objective

Lifecycle failures SHALL preserve architectural correctness.

Incomplete transitions SHALL NEVER corrupt registry state.

17.2 Failure Categories

WF-002 SHALL recognize:

Validation Failure
Compatibility Failure
Authorization Failure
Publication Failure
Transition Failure
Persistence Failure
17.3 Failure Guarantees

Following failure,

the Registry SHALL guarantee:

rollback of incomplete operations;
preservation of previous valid lifecycle state;
immutable failure audit records;
deterministic failure reporting.
17.4 Recovery

After successful recovery,

the workflow SHALL return to the last architecturally consistent lifecycle state.

No partial lifecycle transition SHALL remain visible.

18. Registry Change Governance
18.1 Objective

This section defines the governance model controlling all architectural modifications applied to the Workflow Registry.

Every registry modification SHALL occur through an explicitly governed change process.

Unauthorized architectural changes SHALL NOT occur.

18.2 Governed Change Types

The Registry SHALL govern the following change categories:

Workflow Registration
Metadata Modification
Ownership Transfer
Version Publication
Lifecycle Transition
Capability Update
Dependency Update
Registry Archival

Any additional change category SHALL require architectural approval.

18.3 Change Authority

Every registry modification SHALL identify:

Authorized Actor
Requested Change
Affected Workflow
Justification
Approval Reference

Anonymous architectural modifications SHALL NOT be permitted.

18.4 Change Validation

Before applying any modification, WF-002 SHALL validate:

authorization;
ownership;
version compatibility;
architectural consistency;
dependency integrity.
18.5 Change Guarantees

Every approved change SHALL guarantee:

deterministic execution;
immutable audit evidence;
preservation of architectural integrity;
historical traceability.
19. Metadata Governance
19.1 Objective

WF-002 SHALL maintain authoritative governance over workflow metadata.

Metadata SHALL describe architecture.

Metadata SHALL NOT describe runtime state.

19.2 Mandatory Metadata

Every workflow SHALL define:

Workflow Identifier
Workflow Name
Engine
Category
Owner
Version
Registration Status
Contract Reference
Specification Reference
19.3 Mutable Metadata

Only explicitly designated metadata MAY be modified.

Identity-related metadata SHALL remain immutable.

19.4 Metadata Integrity

Metadata SHALL remain:

complete;
validated;
version-aware;
internally consistent.
20. Capability Governance
20.1 Objective

Capabilities represent architectural services provided by workflows.

Capabilities SHALL be governed independently from implementation.

20.2 Capability Registration

Every capability SHALL declare:

Capability Identifier
Capability Name
Workflow Owner
Version
Contract Reference
20.3 Capability Consistency

Capability definitions SHALL remain unique.

Duplicate capability definitions SHALL NOT exist.

20.4 Capability Evolution

Capability changes SHALL require:

version review;
compatibility validation;
registry approval.
21. Dependency Governance
21.1 Objective

Dependency governance ensures architectural correctness between workflows.

Dependencies SHALL remain explicit throughout the workflow lifecycle.

21.2 Dependency Ownership

Every dependency SHALL identify:

Source Workflow
Target Workflow
Dependency Type
Required Version
21.3 Dependency Integrity

WF-002 SHALL reject dependencies that introduce:

circular references;
orphan references;
incompatible versions;
undefined workflows.
21.4 Dependency Evolution

Dependency modifications SHALL undergo the same governance process as workflow modifications.

22. Registry Policy Enforcement
22.1 Objective

WF-002 SHALL enforce architectural policies uniformly across all registry operations.

Policy enforcement SHALL precede persistence.

22.2 Mandatory Policies

The Registry SHALL enforce:

Identity Policy
Ownership Policy
Version Policy
Capability Policy
Dependency Policy
Lifecycle Policy
Security Policy
22.3 Policy Violations

Policy violations SHALL result in immediate rejection of the requested operation.

Partial policy enforcement SHALL NOT occur.

22.4 Policy Traceability

Every policy decision SHALL become permanently auditable.

23. Registry Decision Model
23.1 Objective

WF-002 SHALL make deterministic architectural decisions regarding registry modifications.

Decision-making SHALL remain transparent and reproducible.

23.2 Decision Outcomes

Every evaluated operation SHALL result in exactly one outcome:

Approved
Rejected
Requires Manual Review

No additional outcome SHALL exist.

23.3 Decision Evidence

Every decision SHALL record:

Decision Identifier
Evaluation Timestamp
Decision Outcome
Decision Basis
Correlation Identifier
23.4 Decision Consistency

Equivalent architectural requests SHALL produce equivalent registry decisions under identical conditions.

24. Phase 2 Compliance Requirements

An implementation SHALL satisfy Phase 2 only if all of the following remain continuously true:

Requirement	Mandatory
Deterministic Registration Lifecycle	SHALL
Controlled Version Governance	SHALL
Explicit Ownership	SHALL
Immutable Identity	SHALL
Capability Governance	SHALL
Dependency Governance	SHALL
Registry Policy Enforcement	SHALL
Complete Audit Trail	SHALL
Architectural Consistency	SHALL

Failure of any mandatory requirement SHALL invalidate Phase 2 compliance.

25. Phase 2 Completion Statement

Phase 2 establishes the complete governance framework for the architectural lifecycle of registered workflows.

It defines how workflows are registered, validated, published, activated, versioned, governed, modified, suspended, deprecated, and archived while preserving architectural integrity, deterministic behavior, and complete auditability.

Upon successful implementation, WF-002 SHALL ensure that every workflow within AHOS evolves through a controlled, policy-driven, and fully traceable lifecycle.
