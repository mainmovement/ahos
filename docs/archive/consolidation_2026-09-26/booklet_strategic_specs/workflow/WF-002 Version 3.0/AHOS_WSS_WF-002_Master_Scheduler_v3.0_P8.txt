AHOS Workflow Specification Sheet
WF-002 — Workflow Registry
Version 1.0
Phase 8 — Observability, Operational Excellence & Future Evolution
1. Purpose
1.1 Objective

This phase establishes the long-term operational principles governing observability, health assessment, architectural metrics, continuous evolution, and future scalability of the Workflow Registry.

The Registry SHALL remain observable, measurable, maintainable, and evolvable throughout the lifetime of the AHOS platform.

1.2 Scope

This phase governs:

Registry Health
Registry Observability
Architectural Metrics
Operational Monitoring
Registry Diagnostics
Evolution Principles
Future Scalability
Operational Excellence

Infrastructure-specific monitoring implementations remain outside this specification.

2. Observability Philosophy

Observability SHALL enable understanding of architectural behavior without altering architectural behavior.

Measurement SHALL NEVER influence registry correctness.

Registry observability SHALL remain passive.

3. Architectural Health Model

The Registry SHALL continuously expose its architectural health.

Architectural health SHALL represent the integrity of registry operations rather than infrastructure availability.

Health SHALL remain deterministic.

4. Registry Health Indicators

At minimum, the Registry SHALL expose:

Registration Health
Validation Health
Discovery Health
Governance Health
Synchronization Health
Consistency Health

Future indicators MAY be introduced through architectural revisions.

5. Architectural Metrics

WF-002 SHALL define architectural metrics including:

Registered Workflow Count
Active Workflow Count
Deprecated Workflow Count
Archived Workflow Count
Validation Success Rate
Validation Failure Rate
Discovery Request Count
Governance Decision Count
Synchronization Events
Registry Consistency Status

Metrics SHALL describe architectural behavior rather than infrastructure utilization.

6. Registry Diagnostics

The Registry SHALL support architectural diagnostics for:

inconsistent metadata;
orphan workflows;
orphan capabilities;
invalid dependencies;
version conflicts;
ownership inconsistencies;
governance violations.

Diagnostics SHALL remain read-only.

7. Operational Excellence Principles

Operational excellence SHALL prioritize:

correctness;
maintainability;
predictability;
transparency;
traceability.

Operational convenience SHALL NEVER compromise architectural integrity.

8. Evolution Principles

WF-002 SHALL evolve through controlled architectural revisions.

Evolution SHALL preserve:

backward compatibility where declared;
immutable workflow identity;
audit history;
published contracts;
architectural consistency.

Evolution SHALL NEVER invalidate historical architectural evidence.

9. Extensibility Model

Future architectural extensions SHALL:

preserve registry authority;
preserve deterministic behavior;
preserve policy enforcement;
preserve governance principles;
preserve contract compatibility.

Extensions SHALL augment architecture rather than replace it.

10. Architectural Maturity

The Workflow Registry SHALL continuously satisfy the characteristics of an Enterprise Architectural Registry:

Single Source of Truth
Deterministic Governance
Immutable Identity
Version Awareness
Architectural Discoverability
Policy Enforcement
Auditability
Technology Independence
Future Scalability

Failure to maintain these characteristics SHALL constitute architectural regression.

11. Future Evolution Readiness

The Registry SHALL remain compatible with future support for:

additional Engines;
additional Workflow Categories;
additional Capability Types;
external architectural services;
distributed governance;
federated registry models;
AI-assisted architectural analysis.

Future evolution SHALL extend the registry without redefining its architectural authority.

12. Architectural Constraints

WF-002 SHALL NEVER evolve into:

a Scheduler;
an Execution Manager;
a Monitoring Engine;
a Configuration Store;
a Business Rules Engine.

Its sole responsibility SHALL remain architectural workflow governance.

13. Registry Quality Attributes

The Workflow Registry SHALL continuously demonstrate:

Determinism
Consistency
Integrity
Auditability
Maintainability
Evolvability
Predictability
Simplicity
Extensibility

These attributes SHALL guide every future architectural revision.

14. Long-Term Architectural Vision

WF-002 SHALL remain a permanent architectural foundation of AHOS.

Regardless of future technology choices, deployment models, or implementation strategies, the Workflow Registry SHALL continue to provide one authoritative representation of workflow architecture.

15. Final Architectural Invariants

The following SHALL remain true throughout the lifetime of the AHOS platform.

Authority Invariant

Exactly one authoritative Workflow Registry SHALL exist.

Identity Invariant

Workflow identities SHALL remain immutable.

Governance Invariant

Every architectural modification SHALL remain governed.

Audit Invariant

Every architectural decision SHALL remain reconstructable.

Consistency Invariant

Architectural knowledge SHALL remain internally consistent.

Discoverability Invariant

Every Active workflow SHALL remain discoverable.

Technology Independence Invariant

Architectural behavior SHALL remain independent from implementation technology.

17. WF-002 Completion Statement

WF-002 establishes the complete architectural governance model for workflow registration within the AHOS platform.

Across eight phases, it defines how workflows are identified, registered, versioned, validated, discovered, governed, synchronized, observed, and evolved while preserving deterministic behavior, architectural consistency, and immutable historical traceability.

The Workflow Registry intentionally separates architectural knowledge from runtime execution, serving as the permanent Authoritative Source of Truth for all workflow definitions.

Every Runtime Engine, Intelligence Engine, Data Acquisition Engine, Governance Component, and future architectural service SHALL rely upon WF-002 as the definitive authority governing workflow architecture.

An implementation SHALL be considered fully compliant with WF-002 Version 1.0 only if every mandatory requirement across all eight phases remains continuously satisfied.

Compliance SHALL include, at minimum:

Violation of any mandatory domain SHALL invalidate enterprise compliance.
