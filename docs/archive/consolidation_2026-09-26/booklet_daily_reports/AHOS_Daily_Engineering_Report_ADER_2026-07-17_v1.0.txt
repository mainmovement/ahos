# 3. Major Decisions Made Today

The following strategic decisions were officially approved.

### Decision 01

The strategic documentation phase has ended.

No additional high-level strategic documents will be created unless absolutely necessary.

---

### Decision 02

Previously approved documents will remain frozen.

Future improvements will be introduced through new document versions rather than modifying historical documents.

---

### Decision 03

AHOS will be developed incrementally.

Each workflow will be designed, implemented, tested, reviewed, frozen, and only then will development proceed to the next workflow.

---

### Decision 04

Each workflow must have one clear responsibility.

Large monolithic workflows are prohibited.

---

### Decision 05

No production modification may occur without explicit operator approval.

Any proposed architectural, workflow, prompt, or configuration improvement must first be submitted through Telegram for review and approval.

---

### Decision 06

The platform shall remain optimized for:

* Single laptop deployment
* Single operator
* Docker
* n8n
* PostgreSQL
* Open-source software
* Low-cost or free AI services whenever practical
* Deployment within Iran under existing restrictions

---

### Decision 07

The primary operational focus of AHOS remains the intelligent discovery, evaluation, simulation, ranking, and monitoring of decentralized early-stage tokens rather than established cryptocurrencies.

# 6. Lessons Learned

Today's engineering session produced several important conclusions.

* Excessive documentation delays implementation.
* Frozen documentation provides long-term architectural stability.
* Modular workflows significantly reduce maintenance complexity.
* Every workflow should have one responsibility only.
* Practical implementation should now become the primary project focus.
* Engineering discipline is more valuable than adding unnecessary complexity.
