---

# 2. Major Decisions Made

## 2.1 Deployment Philosophy Confirmed

A major architectural clarification was finalized.

AHOS is designed for:

* Single operator
* Single laptop deployment
* PostgreSQL
* n8n
* Local services
* No distributed cluster
* No Kubernetes
* No Service Mesh
* No Microservice fleet

This decision significantly influences every future architectural choice.

Enterprise architectural principles are retained where they improve correctness and resilience, but enterprise infrastructure complexity is intentionally avoided.

## 2.3 Saga / Compensation Review

An additional architectural review was requested specifically covering Saga patterns.

The conclusion was:

Traditional Enterprise Saga Architecture is unnecessary.

However,

A lightweight compensation architecture is highly valuable because AHOS contains long-running workflows such as:

Research

↓

Hypothesis

↓

Risk Analysis

↓

Simulation

↓

Recommendation

↓

Execution

Failures during intermediate stages require automatic cleanup.

---

## 2.4 New Architecture Introduced

A complete new section was proposed:

Section 2.9

Workflow Compensation & Recovery Architecture

This introduces:

* Workflow lifecycle tracking
* Compensation event model
* Compensation orchestration
* Resource lease auto-release
* Reverse-order compensation
* Idempotent compensation
* Partial failure handling
* Workflow timeout recovery
* Compensation monitoring
* Compensation metrics
* Operator visibility
* ADR-013

without introducing distributed transaction coordinators.

---

## 2.5 Architectural Principle Established

Compensation is NOT rollback.

Compensation is:

Best-Effort

Idempotent

Audit Preserving

Resource Releasing

State Correcting

This principle becomes a permanent architectural invariant.
